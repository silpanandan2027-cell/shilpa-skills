# My reel style

The daily-reel skill reads this before it animates. Replace the defaults with your own look,
or drop 2-3 of your best reels into `content/style-refs/` and Claude will match them.

- Format: 1080x1920, 30 fps, 30-35 s
- Colours: background #0E0E10, text #FFFFFF, accent #C8FF3D
- Fonts: headings Inter Tight 800, captions Inter 700
- Captions: centred in the lower third, 2-4 words at a time, the spoken word turns accent colour
- Hook (0-2 s): one big line, punch-in zoom
- Steps: a "1 / 2 / 3" counter top-left, one simple visual per step
- CTA: pill button "Comment AI" with a keyboard-tap sound, last 4 s
- Music: low lo-fi bed under the voice, ducked to -22 dB
- Cover: 1080x1920, the hook in 3-5 words, readable in the 1080x1350 grid crop
