Thirteen years.

That's how long it's been since the last Grand Theft Auto, a game that sold more than two
hundred million copies.

And on November 19th, Rockstar finally hands us the sequel: a brand new Vice City, two
outlaws on the run, and a first trailer that set a YouTube record on day one.

So before you spend eighty bucks on it, stay with me. I'm going to run through everything:
the story, the map, the characters, the price, and the stuff Rockstar still isn't telling us.

## 0:32 The long road here

[VISUAL] Timeline card: 2013 → Dec 2023 → May 2025 → Nov 2026.

Quick history lesson. GTA 5 came out in 2013. Let that sink in. People who were in school when
it launched are now paying rent.

Rockstar officially showed GTA 6 in December 2023, and that first trailer became one of the
most watched game reveals ever on YouTube. It was meant to come out in 2025. Then it moved to
May 26th, 2026. Then, one more time, to November 19th, 2026.

And honestly? If the extra time means fewer bugs on day one, I'm fine with that.

## 1:09 Welcome to Leonida

[VISUAL] Map-style card: "STATE OF LEONIDA". Region names pop in one at a time.
[CLIP] Trailer 2: aerial shots of the city and the swamps.

The big one: we're going back to Vice City. But this time Vice City is only part of it.
The whole game takes place in the State of Leonida, which is Rockstar's version of Florida.

You've got Vice City itself, all neon, beaches and money. Then the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers
with its swamps and gators, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, and Mount Kalaga National Park.

What makes this exciting isn't just the size. It's how alive it looks. In the trailers people
film each other on their phones, crowds pack the beach, and there's traffic everywhere. It looks
less like a game map and more like a place that keeps going when you're not looking.

## 1:49 Jason and Lucia

[VISUAL] Two character cards side by side. Neon pink for Lucia, teal for Jason.
[CLIP] Trailer 2: Lucia's prison scene, then Jason.

For the first time in a main GTA game, one of the leads is a woman. Lucia Caminos. We first meet
her in prison, and she does not look like someone who plans on staying there.

Then there's Jason Duval, who has been around this kind of life for a while and wants out. The
two of them team up, and the whole vibe is a modern Bonnie and Clyde story. Two people, one bad
plan after another, and the question of whether they trust each other enough to survive it.

Rockstar's best stories have always been about people. Not explosions. People. And this pair
already feels more personal than anything they've done before.

## 2:30 What it actually costs

[VISUAL] Price card: "$79.99 STANDARD". Small text: "PS5 · Xbox Series X|S".

Okay, money. The standard edition is $79.99. Yes, that's more than most games. It's on PS5 and
Xbox Series X and S. Preloading opened on November 12th, so if you've already bought it digitally,
you can install it right now and be ready the second it unlocks.

The exact unlock time depends on where you live, so check your store page.

## 2:55 The stuff we still don't know

[VISUAL] Big neon question marks. Three lines appear: "PC?", "GTA ONLINE?", "DLC?"

Now the questions. First: PC. No PC release date yet. If you play on PC, history says you'll be
waiting a while.

Second: online. A new GTA Online is coming, but it isn't part of the launch, so day one is all
about the story.

And third: how long will Rockstar support this game? If GTA 5 is anything to go by, the answer
is probably the next ten years.
