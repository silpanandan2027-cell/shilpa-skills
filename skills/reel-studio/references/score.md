# Pre-post score (virality coach)

Score the reel the way a tired viewer scrolling at night would meet it. Look at the first frame, frames every 2-3 seconds, and the last frame; read the script and caption.
Each line is out of 10; the total is the sum of the seven scaled to 100 (sum × 100 ÷ 70), then minus 5 for every technical FIX from `check.py`.

| Part | 10 means | Common reasons for a low score |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** (counts double: add it twice) | Stops the right person in 1.5s, with sound off too | Slow start, greeting, vague words, text appears late, first frame is a logo or blank |
| **Clarity** | One idea, understood on the first watch | Two or three topics, jargon, screen text too long to read in time |
| **Retention** | Something changes every 2-3s, open loop until the payoff | Long static shots, all the value in the first 3 seconds, no reason to stay |
| **Value** | Viewer learns, feels or gets something they'd thank you for | Generic advice, nothing new, all brag no help |
| **Payoff** | The hook's promise is kept, clearly, near the end | Bait-and-switch, payoff buried, ends abruptly |
| **Ask** | One clear action that fits the reel (comment a word, save, follow, DM) | No ask, three asks, "link in bio" with nothing worth clicking |
| **Shareability** | Someone would send it to a friend or save it for later | Only about you, no "this is so me" or "you need this" moment |

How to report
- Total out of 100 and one line per part.
- The three changes that would add the most points, in plain words, most important first.
- If Hook is under 8: write 3 new hooks (different formulas from hooks.md).
- 80+: post it. 70-79: post, or fix the top issue if it is quick. Under 70: fix and re-render first.
- A score is a checklist, not a prediction. Never promise views.
