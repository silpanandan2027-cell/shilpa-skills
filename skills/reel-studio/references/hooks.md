# Hook formulas

The hook is the first 1.5 seconds: the spoken line, the screen text and the first frame together. Its only job is to make the right person stay for second two.
Write 5 per topic with different formulas, score each 1-5 on **curiosity** (do I need the next line?), **clarity** (do I get it with sound off?) and **specificity** (numbers, names, a real situation), and keep the best.

| # | Formula | Shape | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Call-out | "If you [specific situation], [stop/watch this]" | "If your app has users but no buyers, watch this." |
| 2 | Mistake | "You're [doing X] wrong" / "Stop [common habit]" | "Stop posting your product demo first." |
| 3 | Result first | "[Result] in [time] with [surprising method]" | "300 sign-ups in a week from one reel." |
| 4 | Number list | "[N] [things] that [outcome]" | "3 reels every SaaS founder should post." |
| 5 | Contrarian | "[Popular advice] is wrong. Here's why." | "Posting every day is killing your reach." |
| 6 | Secret / insider | "Nobody tells you [truth about niche]" | "Nobody tells you followers don't pay." |
| 7 | Before / after | "I went from [bad] to [good]" | "From 0 to 10k followers without showing my face." |
| 8 | Question | A question the viewer is already asking | "Why does nobody buy after the free trial?" |
| 9 | Story open | Drop into the middle of a moment | "A customer DMed me at 2 a.m. and said..." |
| 10 | Price / money | Money in the first words | "This ₹500 tool replaced my ₹40,000 agency." |
| 11 | Challenge | "I tried [X] for [time]" | "I posted 30 reels in 30 days. Here's the data." |
| 12 | Comparison | "[A] vs [B]" | "ChatGPT vs Claude for writing captions." |
| 13 | Warning | "Don't [do X] until you've seen this" | "Don't launch on Product Hunt before you see this." |
| 14 | How-to promise | "How to [result] without [pain]" | "How to get leads without running ads." |
| 15 | Proof / screenshot | Show the number on screen, say what it means | "This dashboard is why I quit my job." |
| 16 | Myth | "[Belief] is a myth" | "You don't need 10k followers to sell." |
| 17 | POV | "POV: [relatable moment]" | "POV: your first sale comes from a reel." |
| 18 | Specific audience + time | "[Audience], do this before [time]" | "Kerala shop owners, do this before Onam." |
| 19 | Unexpected visual | A strange first frame that needs explaining | A laptop in a fridge: "My servers are this hot." |
| 20 | Reply to a comment | "Someone asked [question]" | "Someone asked if reels work for B2B. They do, here's how." |

Rules
- Say the hook in the first second; no "Hi guys", no logo intro, no slow zoom on nothing.
- Screen text and spoken line should agree but can differ: text is the headline (under 8 words), voice is the sentence.
- Specific beats clever. "3 reels" beats "some reels"; "₹40,000 agency" beats "expensive agency".
- Make a promise the body keeps. A hook that the reel does not pay off kills watch time and trust.
- Malayalam: write it as spoken; mixing in English words people actually use (reel, sale, app) is fine.
